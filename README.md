My app called Animanga Universe is an Android app, which is used to keep track of animes and mangas of each user. It's very similar to MyAnimeList and other apps, but the main difference is that this app is in Spanish (even though for some parts an English translation is available). The Spanish language is the forth most spoken lagnuage in the world, the 3 languaegs above it are English, Hindi and Chinese, but nowdays anime and manga became very popular in the whole world, so it's also neccessary to have an app for spanish speakers.
Tha main goal is to have an app with a giant database of animes and mangas, so the users can save them in different lists and keep track of all of them. Also get to know other titles, or speak and debate on forums about their favorites
My main motivation to create this app was that there are lots of people interested in anime and manga, and from my experience it's a very useful tool to have. As there is no app in Spanish, I have decided to create one
